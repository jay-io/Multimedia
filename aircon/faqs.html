What is the average lifespan of an air conditioning unit?

Typically, an air conditioning unit lasts between 10 to 15 years with proper maintenance.

How often should I service my air conditioning system?

It is recommended to service your air conditioning system at least once a year to ensure optimal performance.

What are the signs that my air conditioning unit needs repair?

Common signs include unusual noises, insufficient cooling, and increased energy bills.
